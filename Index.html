PongResonance
Citadel Clash

Pong with a base to build and a base to break. First to 18 wins.

How to play

Move: drag left and right in the strip at the bottom of the field. When nothing is selected, dragging anywhere on the field works too. Your paddle is green.

Score: get the ball past the other paddle. First to 18 wins.

Energy ⚡: you gain 1 per second, plus 2 per second for each Generator you own. Everything costs energy.

Build: tap a building, then drag on your half of the field and let go to place it. Tap the button again to stop building.

Ball color: a green ball damages their buildings and passes through yours; a red ball does the opposite. The ball takes the color of the last paddle that hit it. After bouncing off a building it switches sides when it crosses the middle line.

Buildings: Wall (5 HP) blocks the ball. Generator makes +2 ⚡ per second. Cannon shoots at their side every 3 s. Repair bay sends a vehicle to fix damaged buildings. Auto drives your paddle for you whenever your finger is off the field.

Powerups: Fireball does 4 damage in an area. Freeze freezes everything of theirs in an area (the ball too) for 3 s and does 1 damage. Drag to aim, let go to fire. Each use raises the price for a while.

Two phones: one phone hosts and shows a 4-letter code, the other joins with it. Both phones need internet.
